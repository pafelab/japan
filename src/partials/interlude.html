記 Field Notes

A page to read at your own pace: the route this story follows, the word it is named for, and a few things worth knowing before you go.

木漏れ日 Komorebi

The word is written 木, tree; 漏れ, leaking; 日, sun. 木tree + 漏れleaking + 日sun

Sunlight leaking through leaves. English needs a sentence for it; Japanese has one word. On a bright day the coins of light beneath a tree are small images of the sun, each gap between the leaves working as a pinhole.

The 日 of komorebi means both sun and day, so this story follows light through one day: dawn mist at Miyajima, overcast calm at Ryōan-ji, komorebi in the bamboo, neon in Shinjuku, three workshops lit by their crafts, and Fuji red at first light.

Before you go

Timetables, tides and customs change: check locally before you travel.

季 When to go

Akafuji 赤富士, the red Fuji, belongs to sunrises in late summer and early autumn, when the snow has gone and the low sun briefly turns the bare slopes red. For a clear view at all, cold winter mornings are the best bet; summer haze often hides the mountain.

礼 Etiquette

Bow before passing under a torii, and walk to one side of the approach: the centre, seichū 正中, is said to be the path of the kami. At the temizuya, rinse the left hand, the right, then your mouth from a cupped palm. Shoes come off on temple verandas; at Ryōan-ji, keep voices low.

鹿 Nara’s deer

The deer of Nara Park are wild and protected. Many bow, mostly in hope of shika-senbei, the deer crackers sold in the park. Bow back, feed them nothing else, and keep maps and bags out of reach: they can nip, butt and tug.

潮 Miyajima’s tides

At high tide the sea fills in around the Ōtorii and the gate seems to float. At low tide the flats drain and you can walk out to it across the sand. The two come roughly six hours apart, so check a tide table before you plan the day.

鉄道 Rail

Kyoto, Nara and Osaka are about an hour or less apart by local and rapid trains that need no reservation. From Shin-Osaka the Tōkaidō Shinkansen runs to Tokyo at up to 285 km/h. For Lake Kawaguchi, limited expresses and highway buses leave from Shinjuku.

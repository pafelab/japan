東京 Hyper-Density

Shinjuku at night, in the rain. A narrow street is lined with stacks of glowing vertical signs for ramen, izakaya, karaoke and yakitori; at its end a giant street screen shows two koi circling in dark water. As the scene ends, the rain slows to a stop, the signs power down, and the night settles into black ink.
